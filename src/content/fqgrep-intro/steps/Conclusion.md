Congratulations — you've completed the `fqgrep` tutorial! 🎉

## What you learned

- **Basic matching** — searching for sequences and counting hits with `-c`
- **Fixed strings vs. regex** — when to use `-F` and how regex metacharacters behave
- **Color and progress** — `--color` and `--progress` for interactive inspection
- **Multiple patterns** — `-e` and pattern files with `-f`
- **Inverted matching** — `-v` to keep reads that *don't* match
- **Paired-end reads** — `--paired` to keep mates together (and the `-v` caveat)
- **Reverse complement** — `--reverse-complement` to search both strands
- **Regular expressions** and **IUPAC ambiguity codes** (`--iupac`)
- **Read-name filtering** with `-N`
- **Protein sequences** with `--protein`
- **Performance** — threads, ordering, compression, and exit codes
